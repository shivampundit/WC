  • Effect of Change of Origin and Scale on Dispersion: If Y = a + bX, then SD(Y) = |b| × SD(X) and Variance(Y) = b² × Variance(X)! All absolute measures of dispersion (Range, QD, MD, SD, Variance) are 100% INDEPENDENT of Change of Origin (addition/subtraction of constant a) and depend ONLY on Change of Scale (multiplication/division by b)!
  • Coefficient of Variation (CV — Karl Pearson's Relative Dispersion): CV = (σ / X̄) × 100 (%). A LOWER CV means the series is MORE consistent, more stable, and more homogeneous (risk per unit of return is lower)!
  • Skewness & Kurtosis Benchmarks: In a Positively Skewed (Right-Tailed) distribution: Mean > Median > Mode and Q₃ - Median > Median - Q₁. In a Negatively Skewed (Left-Tailed) distribution: Mean < Median < Mode. For Kurtosis (Peakedness / Tail Thickness): Normal Curve (Mesokurtic) has β₂ = μ₄ / μ₂² = 3 (Excess Kurtosis γ₂ = β₂ - 3 = 0); Leptokurtic (sharp peak, fat tails — typical of financial asset returns!) has β₂ > 3 (γ₂ > 0); Platykurtic (flat peak, thin tails) has β₂ < 3 (γ₂ < 0)!
