  • Subject F: Costing (Overview, Unit/Job/Batch/Contract/Process/Service Costing, Standard/Marginal/Budgetary Control, Lean System: JIT, Kaizen, 5S, TPM, Six Sigma, BPR)
  • Subject G: Companies Act, 2013 (Chapters III, IV, VIII, X, XI, XII & XXVII — Prospectus, Share Capital, Dividends, Auditors, Directors, Board Powers, NCLT/NCLAT)
  • Subject H: Economics (Demand/Supply, Market Structures, National Income, Keynesian, Multiplier/Accelerator, IS-LM, Phillips Curve, Business Cycles, BoP, Forex, Monetary/Fiscal Policy, NBFCs)
  • Subject J: Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning (Supervised/Unsupervised, Preprocessing, Evaluation, NLP, Sentiment Analysis, Overfitting, LLMs, Agentic AI, Responsible AI, Cross-Validation, RL)
  • Subject K: Media (Micro-Targeting, Consumer Behaviour, Convergence, VR/AR, Digital Divide, Audience Reception, Cultural Representation, OTT, Algorithms, Global Practices, Negotiation)
  • Subject L: Marketing & Communication (Management, Research, Product/Brand, Pricing, Distribution, Promotion, Digital Marketing, Financial Literacy, Soft Skills & EI)

📚 Complete Official Unit-Wise & Topic-Wise Syllabus Breakdown:

  • Unit A — Commerce & Accountancy: Financial Info System, AS (Depreciation, Inventories, Revenue, Fixed Assets, Forex, Investments), Cash/Fund Flow, Ratio Analysis, WACC/Capital Structure/NPV/IRR/CAPM, Share Capital, Bonus/Rights, ESOPs, Buy-Back, Final Accounts
  • Unit B — Management: Nature & Scope, Management Processes (Planning, Organization, Staffing, Directing, Controlling), Role of Manager, Leadership Styles & Theories, HRD, Motivation, Morale & Incentives, Communication & Role of IT
  • Unit C — Finance: Financial System & Regulators (India & World: RBI, SEBI, IRDAI, PFRDA, IMF, World Bank), Primary/Secondary/Commodity Markets, Mutual Funds, Derivatives, Credit Rating Agencies, Financial Inclusion, PPP, Taxes/GST/FRBM, Inflation (WPI/CPI), Sensitivity & Scenario Analysis
  • Unit D — Data Analytics & Statistics (Exclusive): Central Tendency (AM, GM, HM, Median, Mode), Dispersion (QD, MD, SD, CV), Skewness & Kurtosis, Box Plot, Correlation & Regression, Sampling Methods/Funnel/Variation/Distribution, Central Limit Theorem, Confidence Intervals, Hypothesis Testing (Z, t, Chi-Square, ANOVA), Operations Research & Linear Programming, 4 Types of Data Analytics (Descriptive, Diagnostic, Predictive, Prescriptive) & Data Visualization
  • Unit E — Companies Act, 2013 & The Indian Trusts Act, 1882 (Exclusive): Companies Act Chapters III, IV, VIII, X, XI, XII, XXVII + Indian Trusts Act, 1882 (Sections 1–96: Creation, 3 Certainties, Duties/Liabilities/Rights/Disabilities of Trustees, Beneficiary Rights, Extinction & NPS Trust Regulations 2015)
  • Unit F — Economics: Demand/Supply, Market Structures, National Income, Classical & Keynesian, Consumption/Investment, Multiplier/Accelerator, IS-LM, Phillips Curve, Business Cycles, BoP, Forex, Monetary/Fiscal Policy, NBFCs
  • Unit G — Pension Sector of India & World: Status of Pension Sector, Retirement Schemes, NPS, APY, Annuity Plans, Basics of Investment Decision Making & Portfolio Management (Sharpe, Treynor, Jensen's Alpha)

✍️ Phase II Paper 1: Descriptive English Master Guide (Essay, Precis, Comprehension & Business Correspondence)

Common across all 4 exams (100 Marks, 60 Minutes typed on keyboard using CBT editor):

  • 1. Essay Writing (30–40 Marks | 300–400 Words): Use the 5-Paragraph Regulatory Framework: (i) Hook + Statutory/Macro Definition; (ii) Historical & Economic Context with RBI/SEBI/IRDAI/PFRDA/IFSCA data; (iii) Key Reforms & Government Initiatives (Bima Trinity, GIFT IFSC, UPS 2025, Digital Public Infrastructure); (iv) Structural Challenges & Risks; (v) Way Forward aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047.
  • 2. Precis Writing (30–35 Marks | 1/3rd of Original Passage): Always provide a crisp 5–7 word Title at the top; summarize strictly in your own words in a single connected paragraph (or 2 short paragraphs) without first-person pronouns, direct quotes, or outside examples, staying within ±5 words of the 1/3rd target count.
  • 3. Reading Comprehension & Business/Office Correspondence (30–35 Marks): Answer RC questions by paraphrasing the passage's core analytical argument rather than copy-pasting lines verbatim. For IRDAI Paper I Business/Office Correspondence, use formal regulatory letter/memo formatting (Sender Designation, Date, Receiver Designation, Subject Line, 3 concise paragraphs: Context, Core Action/Directive, Closing).
