  • Mandatory Dematerialization for Public & Private Companies (Sec 29 & PAS Rules 2024–2026): Under Section 29(1A) and Rule 9B of the Companies (Prospectus and Allotment of Securities) Rules, all Public Companies and all Private Companies (other than Small Companies) must issue securities exclusively in dematerialized form and facilitate dematerialization of all existing securities via NSDL/CDSL.

Dividend out of Free Reserves in Year of Inadequate/Absence of Profits (Rule 3 of Declaration & Payment of Dividend Rules, 2014 — 4 Mandatory Conditions):
Condition 1 (Rate Cap): Declared Dividend Rate ≤ Average of dividend rates declared in 3 immediately preceding FYs (not applicable if no dividend was declared in each of the 3 preceding FYs)
Condition 2 (Withdrawal Cap): Total amount drawn from accumulated profits ≤ 1/10th (10%) of (Paid-up Share Capital + Free Reserves) as per latest audited financial statements
Condition 3 (First Set-Off): Amount drawn must FIRST be utilized to set off losses incurred in the current financial year before declaring any dividend on equity shares
Condition 4 (Minimum Residual Balance): Balance of reserves after such withdrawal ≥ 15% of Paid-up Share Capital as per latest audited financial statements
Dividend Payment Timeline Chain (Sec 123–127): Day 0 (Declaration) → Within 5 Days (Deposit in Separate Scheduled Bank A/c) → Within 30 Days (Pay/Post Warrant) → Within 7 Days after 30 Days (Day 37: Transfer unpaid to Unpaid Dividend A/c; default interest 12% p.a.) → After 7 Years (Transfer to IEPF)
🧮 Worked Numerical / Practical Case Study: Worked Numerical (Rule 3 — Dividend out of Free Reserves during Loss Year): Omega Ltd has Paid-up Equity Capital = ₹200 Lakh and Free Reserves = ₹100 Lakh (Total = ₹300 Lakh). It incurred a current-year loss (after depreciation) of ₹5 Lakh. Its dividend rates in the preceding 3 years were 10%, 12%, and 14%. What is the maximum dividend rate it can declare? (1) Condition 1 (Rate Cap): Average of 3 yrs = (10+12+14)/3 = 12%; (2) Condition 2 (10% Withdrawal Cap): Max withdrawal = 10% of ₹300L = ₹30 Lakh; (3) Condition 4 (15% Residual Reserve Balance): Reserves after withdrawal must be ≥ 15% of Paid-up Capital (15% of ₹200L = ₹30L), so max withdrawal allowed by Condition 4 = ₹100L − ₹30L = ₹70L. Thus max withdrawal = min(₹30L, ₹70L) = ₹30 Lakh. (4) Condition 3 (Set off Current Loss): Deduct ₹5 Lakh loss first → ₹25 Lakh available for dividend = 12.5% of ₹200L, capped by Condition 1 at 12% (₹24 Lakh)!
